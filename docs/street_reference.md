# Street reference: Toktonalieva 77 detail zone, Bishkek

Site: 42.845424, 74.591755. Compiled 2026-09-24 from web sources only. No images were downloaded; the links point to the originals.

**Evidence tags used below**
- **[E]** The linked page states this. The date is the article date.
- **[T]** Only the headline was seen. The page itself could not be read: AKIpress pages return unrelated text to the fetcher, and 24.kg / YouTube pages sometimes 404 or rate-limit.
- **[D]** Derived by arithmetic from [E] numbers.
- **[A]** Design assumption (Soviet/CIS norms and my own judgement). Replace these with imagery measurements where possible.

**Parking rule:** none of the sources shows kerbside parking on the zone's streets. **Do not model any street parking** unless you see it on imagery. The kerb lane of Akhunbaev and Aitmatov is now a bus lane (see 1.2 and 1.3).

## 0. Where the site actually is

- **Toktonalieva** (former Dushanbinskaya) runs N–S at lon ≈ 74.592, and house numbers increase southward.
  - No. 61, a 12-storey brick block, is at 42.8469 ([2GIS](https://2gis.kg/bishkek/geo/15763234351133774)).
  - The Akhunbaev × Toktonalieva crossing is at 42.8434, 74.5919 ([2GIS](https://2gis.kg/bishkek/geo/70030076161457504)).
  - So **No. 77 is about 200 m north of Akhunbaev, on the block between Akhunbaev and Aini.** [D]
  - The street ends in the north at Mederova, over a 2019 bridge across the **Ala-Archa river** ([kaktus](https://kaktus.media/doc/397743_most_cherez_reky_ala_archa_nakonec_dostroiat._yje_zakatyvaut_asfalt_foto.html)). It crosses Akhunbaev, Aini and Zhamanbaev ([geodzen](https://geodzen.com/kg/bishkek/toktonaliyev)).
- **Around the site:**
  - About 280 m W: the KSTU "Politech" campus, Aitmatov 66, at 42.8450, 74.5884 ([2GIS](https://2gis.kg/bishkek/firm/70000001019349133)). Aitmatov avenue is about 0.5 km W.
  - SE: **Atatürk park**, centroid 42.8395, 74.5958, address K. Shakirov 31 ([2GIS](https://2gis.kg/bishkek/geo/15763285890564106)).
  - About 0.7 km E: the **Ala-Archa river** along Maldybaev ([2GIS point 42.8396, 74.6001](https://2gis.kg/bishkek/geo/70030076134076637); [geodzen Maldybaev](https://geodzen.com/kg/bishkek/maldybayev)).
- **What the neighbourhood is called:** real-estate sites call the surrounding quarters "**Ата-Тюрк парк**" and "**Душанбинка**". Listings there are khrushchyovkas, 104-series blocks, hotel-type blocks and new "elitka" buildings ([lalafo Ata-Turk park](https://lalafo.kg/bishkek/kvartiry/prodazha-kvartir/ata-tjurk-park); [house.kg Vizit](https://www.house.kg/jilie-kompleksy/vizit)). "**Нижний Джал (Джал-23)**" lies SW, around Akhunbaeva 67/1 ([house.kg](https://www.house.kg/en/jilie-kompleksy/dzhal-akhunbaeva)).
- **The microdistricts named in the brief are not next to the site.**
  - No numbered microdistrict (3–12) adjoins the site in any source.
  - Asanbai (SE) and Vostok-5 (NE) are several km away [A: general knowledge]. For yard typology, use the local 4–5-storey stock described in section 3.
- **Street names:** the Aug-2026 renaming leaves Toktonalieva, Akhunbaev, Aini and Zhamanbaev unchanged. Aitmatov now also covers former Manas avenue, and Masaliev became part of Tokombaev avenue ([economist.kg](https://economist.kg/transport/2026/08/26/bishkek-pereimenovanie-ulits-spisok/)).

## 2. Canal / river section

**Finding: there is no Big Chui Canal channel next to Akhunbaev in the 1 km zone per the sources. The bridge the brief refers to is most likely the Akhunbaev–Maldybaev bridge over the Ala-Archa river.** Verify on imagery. The evidence:

- **Ala-Archa river:**
  - It lies about 0.7 km E, and Maldybaev street "is divided by the Ala-Archa" ([geodzen](https://geodzen.com/kg/bishkek/maldybayev)).
  - Maldybaeva between Akhunbaeva and "the nearest bridge" has a broken sidewalk and a **gap in the river railing** ("Не упади" is written by the gap) ([kaktus 12.06.2026](https://kaktus.media/doc/547857_na_yl._maldybaeva_ychastok_trotyara_razryshen._jiteli_prosiat_ego_otremontirovat_video.html)). [E]
  - Headlines only: "Где устанавливают ограждения вдоль реки Ала-Арча по улице Малдыбаева?" and "Вдоль реки на Малдыбаева до сих пор не установлены ограждения" ([1](https://reporter.akipress.org/news:223214?from=rss), [2](https://reporter.akipress.org/news:222725?from=rss)). [T]
  - The Toktonalieva–Mederova bridge crosses the same river: 36 m long, 21.5 m wide, 2019. [E]
- **East BChK (ВБЧК)** runs E–W east of the river:
  - The Shukurov-street bridge (lon ≈ 74.627) links Akhunbaev to Mederova over the ВБЧК. It started in 2024 and is **15 m wide including the footway** ([bishkek.gov.kg](https://www.bishkek.gov.kg/ru/post/28857); [24.kg](https://24.kg/obschestvo/293278_stroitelstvo_mosta_cherez_vostochnyiy_bchk_vbishkeke_oboydetsya_v23milliona_somov/)). ## 5. Uncertainties and what to check on imagery

1. **The canal in the brief.** Confirm whether the waterway at the Akhunbaev bridge is the Ala-Archa (as the evidence suggests) or a ВБЧК branch. Measure channel width, bank type (wall, gabion or slab) and railing.
2. **Lane counts and medians** on Toktonalieva, Akhunbaev, Aini and Zhamanbaev. No source gives them. Check the bus-lane marking on Akhunbaev and Aitmatov.
3. **Aitmatov north of Akhunbaev** has been under reconstruction since Jul 2026. Choose the modelled date: construction site or the 2025 southern profile mirrored.
4. **Aryk position and state** on Toktonalieva near No. 77. Is it in front of or behind the sidewalk, lotok or earth, and are there crossing slabs at gates?
5. **Parking.** Mark only cars you actually see on current imagery, in yards and off-street lots (e.g. the lot at No. 61). Kerb lanes stay empty.
6. **Tree rows.** Measure crown spacing and whether the Aitmatov oak row is gone (118 oaks felled in 2025).
7. **What is at No. 77.** Is it KSTU land, a residential block or a new ЖК? No listing for No. 77 was found. Check the frontage type (fence, shops, forecourt).
8. **Soviet block heights.** The local stock is largely 4-storey. Verify each footprint (from shadows or 2GIS cards).
9. **Old images.** Street View (2016) and pre-2024 photos show surfaces from before the 2024–26 repaving and pavement works; do not copy their surfaces.
10. **AKIpress [T] items.** Only headlines were verified. Open them manually if their photos matter: the Akhunbaev–Maldybaev bridge, the river railings and the Aitmatov fences.
